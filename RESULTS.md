## 1. Core benchmark (VQC vs. classical baselines)

**Status: finalized.**

Real ground-truth RSNA Knee data, not a synthetic benchmark. **n=58** is the
actual labeled ceiling in this competition dataset — the count of studies
with full 12-condition label coverage *and* a usable DICOM series on disk
(via [`qknee.data.dataset.RSNAKneeDataset`](qknee/data/dataset.py)). Two
conditions (Lateral OA, Baker's) had zero positive-or-negative coverage
across all 58 studies and are excluded from every score below, leaving 10
scored conditions.

Generated by [`qknee/models/evaluate.py`](qknee/models/evaluate.py)'s
`export_kaggle_benchmark_summary` →
[`qknee/artifacts/kaggle_benchmark_summary.json`](qknee/artifacts/kaggle_benchmark_summary.json).
Final Score = macro-averaged ROC-AUC across scored conditions.

**10 conditions (all scored conditions, Effusion included)**

| Model                              | Final Score (macro-AUC) | ACL    | MCL    | Med. Meniscus | Lat. Meniscus | Medial OA | PF OA  | Effusion | Synovitis | Contusion | Fracture |
|-------------------------------------|:------------------------:|:------:|:------:|:--------------:|:---------------:|:---------:|:------:|:--------:|:---------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| Baseline Classical ResNet18         | **0.6732**               | 0.6607 | 0.7500 | 0.8333          | 0.1111          | 1.0000    | 0.7273 | 0.8148   | 0.5536    | 0.7407    | 0.5400   |
| Classical ResNet18 + RBF SVM        | 0.5944                   | 0.5893 | 0.5227 | 0.7593          | 0.1111          | 0.9615    | 0.6136 | 0.9074   | 0.5357    | 0.4630    | 0.4800   |
| Hybrid Q-Knee VQC                   | 0.5632                   | 0.5536 | 0.5682 | 0.5000          | 0.3889          | 0.9231    | 0.7273 | 0.6111   | 0.6786    | 0.4815    | 0.2000   |

**9 conditions (excl. Effusion — controls for the label-quality issue in
[§2](#2-effusion-label-quality-audit))**

| Model                              | Final Score (macro-AUC) | Δ vs. baseline |
|-------------------------------------|:------------------------:|:---------------:|
| Baseline Classical ResNet18         | **0.6574**               | —                |
| Classical ResNet18 + RBF SVM        | 0.5596                   | −0.098           |
| Hybrid Q-Knee VQC                   | 0.5579                   | −0.100           |

With Effusion included, VQC trails the ResNet18 baseline by 0.110 (0.5632 vs.
0.6732). With Effusion excluded, that gap narrows to 0.100 (0.5579 vs.
0.6574) — the RBF-SVM baseline, which loses the most from dropping the noisy
label, ends up statistically indistinguishable from VQC (0.5596 vs. 0.5579).
At n=58, none of these differences should be read as more than directional —
this is a small-sample real-data check, not a validated clinical benchmark.

Core 3-condition subset (ACL, MCL, Medial Meniscus — unaffected by the
Effusion exclusion, so identical in both views): ResNet18 0.7480, RBF-SVM
0.6238, VQC 0.5406.

## 2. Effusion label-quality audit

**Status: finalized.**

On the raw 10-condition score in [§1](#1-core-benchmark-vqc-vs-classical-baselines),
VQC trails both classical baselines. An audit of the ground-truth Effusion
labels found a real label-quality problem, not a modeling one: the same
mild/small/trace-severity report phrasing — including 5 cases of
verbatim-identical text — is mapped to opposite labels (0 and 1) in ~43%
(25/58) of studies.

This means Effusion's contribution to every model's macro-AUC in the
10-condition table is partly measuring which model happened to fit the noise
in a near-coin-flip label, not real predictive signal. Dropping Effusion and
recomputing over the remaining 9 conditions is the controlled comparison —
see the second table in [§1](#1-core-benchmark-vqc-vs-classical-baselines) —
and the VQC-vs-ResNet18 gap narrows accordingly (0.110 → 0.100), with the
RBF-SVM baseline (which loses the most from the drop) becoming statistically
indistinguishable from VQC.

**Takeaway:** report the 9-condition view as the primary comparison; keep the
10-condition view only as a reference point for what changes when the known
label defect is included. Do not re-label or "fix" the Effusion ground truth
without going back to the original radiology reports — the fix here is
exclusion, not imputation.

## 3. SSL pretraining — seeded multi-run result

**Status: finalized.**

`scripts/run_ssl_vs_imagenet_kfold.py` previously constructed the VQC head
(`VQCClassifier`, whose quantum `TorchLayer` weights and readout
`nn.Linear` are both randomly initialized from torch's global RNG) without
seeding torch first, so per-fold weight init silently depended on whatever
RNG state prior folds/conditions happened to leave behind — not
reproducible, and not attributable to `--seed`. This is now fixed:
`train_vqc_with_curve` calls `torch.manual_seed(init_seed)` immediately
before constructing the model, with `init_seed` derived deterministically
from `--seed` (per fold for the epoch-curve analysis, per condition+fold for
the out-of-fold macro-AUC analysis). Without this fix, running the same
`--seed` twice would not reproduce the same VQC init, so a multi-seed sweep
would not actually be measuring seed-to-seed variance — it would be
measuring uncontrolled RNG-state variance. This fix is what makes the sweep
below meaningful.

A single earlier run (retracted — see the note in
[README.md](README.md#reproducing-the-ssl-pretrained-backbone)) also used a
pretext-task preprocessing bug (`Resize` instead of `RandomResizedCrop`)
that let the rotation-prediction task shortcut on the fixed position of
black background at the image edges rather than learning real anatomy; that
bug is fixed in [`qknee/models/ssl_pretrain.py`](qknee/models/ssl_pretrain.py)
and the corrected backbone/checkpoint is `resnet18_ssl_backbone_fixed.*`.

Ran the comparison 5 times with explicit seeds 0–4 (`--seed 0` … `--seed
4`), each seed producing one full `imagenet_only` vs. `ssl_pretrained`
comparison (5-fold CV, ACL epoch-curve + 12-condition out-of-fold macro-AUC).
Per-seed results:

| Seed | ImageNet peak_epoch | SSL peak_epoch | ImageNet oof_macro_auc | SSL oof_macro_auc |
|:----:|:--------------------:|:---------------:|:------------------------:|:--------------------:|
| 0    | 13                    | 25               | 0.5296                    | 0.5053                |
| 1    | 32                    | 0                | 0.5072                    | 0.5070                |
| 2    | 3                     | 7                | 0.5473                    | 0.4985                |
| 3    | 15                    | 14               | 0.5170                    | 0.5108                |
| 4    | 1                     | 12               | 0.5200                    | 0.5466                |

**Spread across seeds (mean / min / max / std):**

| Metric | Backbone | Mean | Min | Max | Std |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| peak_epoch | ImageNet-only | 12.8 | 1 | 32 | 12.34 |
| peak_epoch | SSL-pretrained | 11.6 | 0 | 25 | 9.24 |
| oof_macro_auc | ImageNet-only | 0.5242 | 0.5072 | 0.5473 | 0.0152 |
| oof_macro_auc | SSL-pretrained | 0.5137 | 0.4985 | 0.5466 | 0.0190 |

**Finding: no reliable difference detected at this sample size (58 studies,
5 seeds).**

- `oof_macro_auc` spreads overlap almost completely: ImageNet-only's full
  range (0.5072–0.5473) sits inside SSL-pretrained's full range
  (0.4985–0.5466), and the ~0.010 gap between the two means is an order of
  magnitude smaller than either backbone's own seed-to-seed std (0.015–0.019).
  Both backbones also hover within ~0.02–0.05 of chance (0.50) on average —
  this dataset, at n=58, is not distinguishing "learned a real 12-condition
  signal" from "learned very little" for either backbone, let alone
  distinguishing the two backbones from each other.
- `peak_epoch` is even noisier and shows no consistent pattern: SSL peaks
  earlier than ImageNet in 3/5 seeds (0, 2, 4) and later in 1/5 (seed 0), and
  ties are absent — the std (9–12 epochs) is comparable to the mean itself,
  meaning "which epoch the curve happens to peak at" on a 58-sample, 5-fold
  curve is dominated by fold-composition and init noise, not by which
  backbone was used.
- This is a real, reportable result, not a failed experiment: it means the
  SSL-pretraining question ("does further self-supervised pretraining on
  unlabeled knee MRI improve the downstream VQC pipeline over plain
  ImageNet weights?") cannot be answered either way from this 58-study
  labeled set with 5 seeds — the honest conclusion is "not detectable here,"
  not "SSL doesn't help" or "SSL helps." A larger labeled set (or many more
  seeds, which only shrinks the standard error of the *mean*, not the
  fold-composition noise driving `peak_epoch`) would be needed to say more.

Regenerate with:

```bash
for s in 0 1 2 3 4; do
  PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/run_ssl_vs_imagenet_kfold.py \
    --ssl-checkpoint qknee/artifacts/resnet18_ssl_backbone_fixed.pt \
    --seed "$s" \
    --output "qknee/artifacts/ssl_vs_imagenet_kfold_summary_seed${s}.json"
done
```

## 4. Parameter efficiency comparison (VQC vs. matched MLP)

**Status: finalized.**

`scripts/run_vqc_vs_mlp_kfold.py` compares `VQCClassifier(n_qubits=4,
n_layers=3)` against `MatchedMLP` (`Linear(4, 8, bias=False) → ReLU →
Linear(8, 1, bias=True)`) — a classical head with the **exact same number
of trainable parameters (41)**, verified programmatically at runtime
(`_assert_param_counts_match`) rather than assumed. Both models consume the
same PCA(4)-reduced ImageNet-ResNet18 features, with the same per-fold-fit
`QuantumDimReducer`, the same seeded 5-fold `StratifiedKFold`, and the same
`torch.manual_seed`-per-fold/per-condition init fix as
[§3](#3-ssl-pretraining--seeded-multi-run-result), run across the same 5
explicit seeds (0–4) for consistency with that sweep.

The question this answers: does the 4-qubit variational circuit itself buy
anything over a classical model with an identical parameter budget, or is
the VQC indistinguishable from what any other 41-parameter model would get
on 58 samples? (Note: `vqc`'s numbers here are, as expected, identical to
`imagenet_only`'s in [§3](#3-ssl-pretraining--seeded-multi-run-result) per
seed — same features, same architecture, same seeding scheme — which is a
useful cross-script consistency check on the seeding fix rather than a
coincidence.)

Per-seed results:

| Seed | VQC peak_epoch | MLP peak_epoch | VQC oof_macro_auc | MLP oof_macro_auc |
|:----:|:---------------:|:----------------:|:--------------------:|:--------------------:|
| 0    | 13               | 6                | 0.5296                | 0.5207                |
| 1    | 32               | 153              | 0.5072                | 0.5422                |
| 2    | 3                | 0                | 0.5471                | 0.5409                |
| 3    | 15               | 0                | 0.5170                | 0.5845                |
| 4    | 1                | 143              | 0.5200                | 0.5321                |

**Spread across seeds (mean / min / max / std):**

| Metric | Model | Mean | Min | Max | Std |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| peak_epoch | VQC | 12.8 | 1 | 32 | 12.34 |
| peak_epoch | MLP (matched) | 60.4 | 0 | 153 | 80.08 |
| oof_macro_auc | VQC | 0.5242 | 0.5072 | 0.5471 | 0.0151 |
| oof_macro_auc | MLP (matched) | 0.5441 | 0.5207 | 0.5845 | 0.0242 |

**Finding: no reliable difference detected at this sample size (58 studies,
5 seeds)** — with one caveat below worth flagging rather than burying.

- The matched MLP's mean `oof_macro_auc` (0.5441) is nominally ~0.020 higher
  than the VQC's (0.5242), and unlike [§3](#3-ssl-pretraining--seeded-multi-run-result)'s
  near-zero gap, this one is a real, directionally-consistent-looking
  number. But it does not survive a seed-by-seed check: the per-seed
  advantage **flips sign** — VQC wins on seeds 0 and 2, MLP wins on seeds 1,
  3, and 4 — and the paired seed-by-seed differences (MLP − VQC:
  −0.0089, +0.0350, −0.0062, +0.0675, +0.0121) have a standard deviation
  (≈0.032) larger than their own mean (≈0.020), i.e. a paired t-test would
  not clear significance at 5 seeds (t ≈ 1.4 on 4 df, well short of the
  ≈2.78 needed at p<0.05). The full oof_macro_auc ranges also overlap
  (VQC's max 0.5471 sits inside MLP's range 0.5207–0.5845).
- `peak_epoch` shows no usable pattern at all: the matched MLP's std (80.08)
  exceeds its own mean (60.4), swinging from epoch 0 to epoch 153 across
  seeds with no relation to the VQC's peak epoch on the same seed/fold
  split. This says more about ReLU-MLP training dynamics on a tiny,
  4-feature input being erratic than about anything comparable between the
  two architectures.
- Caveat, stated plainly rather than smoothed over: a ~0.02 mean AUC edge
  for the matched MLP, with 3 of 5 seeds favoring it, is a *pattern worth
  re-checking with more seeds*, not proof of nothing. The honest summary is
  that 5 seeds on 58 samples cannot distinguish "the matched MLP has a
  small real edge" from "seed noise happened to favor it more often than
  not" — both are consistent with the data above. Treat this as inconclusive
  with a mild lean toward the classical head, not as evidence the VQC is
  competitive with a parameter-matched classical baseline.

Regenerate with:

```bash
for s in 0 1 2 3 4; do
  PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/run_vqc_vs_mlp_kfold.py \
    --seed "$s" \
    --output "qknee/artifacts/vqc_vs_mlp_kfold_summary_seed${s}.json"
done
```
